Recovery is real for FBA sellers; largely captured by ad spend for the median seller.
5-YEAR CUMULATIVE (2021 → 2026)
Total Amazon has reduced take rate every year for 5 years. Ad spend has risen every year for 5 years. 24.7% –3.0pp 7.7% +1.9pp 67.0% –0.7pp 54.3% 75.5%
Of the 3.0pp cumulative take rate reduction, 1.9pp was absorbed by TACoS growth. Net proceeds improvement: –0.7pp (net worse). Fee policy has not delivered sustained proceeds improvement at the median level.
